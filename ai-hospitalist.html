What it does

A second clinical reader that never loses the thread.

The system analyzes available clinical records: admission documentation, medical histories, medications, laboratory results, diagnostic studies, specialist recommendations, and daily clinical changes.

It identifies potentially overlooked clinical conditions, medication-related concerns, treatment inconsistencies, important unanswered clinical questions, and opportunities to improve diagnostic specificity. Its review accompanies the physician throughout the hospitalization.

Clinical safety

Overlooked chart information, medication discrepancies, potential contraindications, and clinically relevant questions for physician assessment.

Prospective CDI

Clinically supported opportunities for diagnostic specificity, severity documentation, and potential DRG/CC/MCC implications, at the point of care.

Decision support

Evidence-supported considerations and high-value questions, so the encounter focuses on what changes management.

Physician in control

The physician evaluates findings, answers clarification questions, and approves documentation. No autonomous treatment, ordering, or EHR write-back.

The physician workflow

Before rounds. After rounds. Before the note is signed.

01 · BEFORE ROUNDING

Ingest and prioritize

The AI reviews available patient information, identifies potentially overlooked safety concerns, and suggests non-routine, high-value questions for the physician to investigate during the encounter.

02 · AFTER ROUNDING

Reassess with new information

The physician contributes updated history, examination findings, and clinical decisions. The system integrates them into its assessment and identifies additional questions or concerns where appropriate.

03 · BEFORE FINALIZATION

Combined safety and CDI review

The AI runs clinical safety and CDI checks together, surfaces actionable findings, and prepares a physician-reviewed note. The physician remains responsible for clinical judgment, treatment decisions, and final documentation.

Intended benefits

Fewer misses. Better notes. Less administrative drag.

  • Help physicians recognize clinically significant information that may be missed during routine chart review.
  • Improve clinical decision-making and care coordination.
  • Identify medication, diagnostic, and treatment concerns.
  • Increase the accuracy, specificity, and completeness of physician documentation.
  • Reduce avoidable retrospective CDI queries by resolving documentation issues prospectively.
  • Reduce administrative burden while maintaining physician oversight.

Benefits describe design intent. Reductions in query volume, documentation precision, and any financial impact must be measured in deployment studies.

Technical foundation

Designed for real clinical workflow, inside an authorized environment.

Patient information stays inside the authorized clinical environment. External medical knowledge is retrieved using focused clinical concepts, never patient identifiers.

EHR connection

Read-only Epic SMART on FHIR integration; normalized encounters, medications, allergies, diagnoses, laboratory results, notes, reports, and orders.

Existing clinical intelligence

Preserves the physician-developed AI Hospitalist instructions, standard-of-care review, prospective CDI behavior, and existing CDI logic. No replacement clinical rules invented.

Clinical knowledge

Planned or developing connectors for RxNorm, DailyMed, openFDA, PubMed, and society guidelines; configurable institutional pathways for UpToDate and OpenEvidence where supported.

Clinician control

The physician evaluates findings, answers clarification questions, and approves documentation. No autonomous treatment, ordering, or production EHR write-back is claimed.

Engineering status

A working prototype has retrieved and displayed synthetic patients from the Epic developer sandbox through a live, read-only connection, including patient retrieval, normalization, orders, clinical reports, and a clinician interface. The physician-authored clinical engines are being integrated. Production clinical effectiveness has not yet been validated.
