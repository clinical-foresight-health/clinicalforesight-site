Clinical Foresight AI, Inc. joins the NVIDIA Inception program

Physician-founded healthcare AI company accepted into NVIDIA's program for startups building with AI, supporting development of its AI Hospitalist and Autonomous CDI platforms.

Member, NVIDIA Inception program

CHERRY HILL, N.J. — November 3, 2026 — Clinical Foresight AI, Inc., a physician-founded company developing AI-driven clinical decision support and Clinical Documentation Integrity (CDI) technology for hospitals, today announced that it has been accepted into the NVIDIA Inception program.

NVIDIA Inception supports startups that are transforming industries with AI and accelerated computing. Membership gives Clinical Foresight access to NVIDIA's technical expertise, developer resources, and startup ecosystem as the company advances its two products: the AI Hospitalist + Prospective CDI, a physician-facing assistant that surfaces unrecognized clinical findings and documentation gaps while the patient is still in the hospital, and Autonomous Retrospective CDI, a phased automation path for CDI teams.

“Hospitals don't have a data problem; they have a noticing problem. The signal is usually in the chart, it just isn't seen in time. Joining NVIDIA Inception gives us the technical backing to build inference that is fast enough, and reliable enough, to change that on a working hospital floor.”

Daniel L. Francis, DO — Founder, Clinical Foresight AI, Inc.

Clinical Foresight is led by a practicing hospitalist with 15 years in hospital medicine and a background in utilization management and CDI program leadership. The company's platforms are currently in development, with prototype integration against an EHR sandbox environment. No system is deployed in production patient care at this time.

Health systems, CDI leaders, and informatics teams interested in a design partnership or demonstration can reach the company at DrFrancis@clinicalforesight.ai.

About Clinical Foresight AI, Inc.

Clinical Foresight AI, Inc. is a physician-founded healthcare AI company based in Cherry Hill, New Jersey. It builds clinical decision support, patient-safety, and Clinical Documentation Integrity technology designed for the realities of the hospital floor. Learn more at clinicalforesight.ai.

About the NVIDIA Inception program

NVIDIA Inception is a free program that guides AI startups through the NVIDIA platform and ecosystem. Open to AI startups at any stage, it offers members training, reduced-cost access to NVIDIA products, partner offers, and introductions to investors. Learn more at nvidia.com/startups.
